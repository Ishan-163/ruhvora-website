About This Fragrance

Ruhvora Blush Babe For Her is a premium Eau De Parfum crafted for the modern woman who embraces elegance and femininity. This long-lasting fragrance opens with a refreshing burst of Mandarin Orange and Orange Blossom, transitioning into a romantic heart of Violet, juicy Peach, and lush floral notes. Settling into a warm, sophisticated base of Sandalwood, Musk, and sweet Vanilla, it offers an all-day freshness that makes it the perfect signature scent for any occasion or a beautiful gift.
